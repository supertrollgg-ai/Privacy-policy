Privacy Policy — Darthoreon's World: The Clowder Of Travesty Last updated: October 2026 Darthoreon's World: The Clowder Of Travesty is a storybook game made with love for Nanna Verdy and her family. The game itself collects nothing — this policy explains what changes when the optional free-to-play ads are on. 1. The game we make The storybook has no analytics, no tracking, no online accounts, and no servers of ours. It does not send your progress or any personal information to us — ever. On Android, ads and the one-time "remove ads" purchase are handled entirely by Google's own systems, described below. The web (browser) version shows no ads and contains no purchases. 2. Advertising (Android app only) The Android app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's ad SDK collects device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), coarse location signals from those identifiers, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, a Google consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are served. Ads never interrupt a puzzle — only between levels — and the "remove ads" purchase switches them off permanently. Google's use of advertising data is covered by their own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). 3. Purchases (Android app only) The one-time "remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details; Google shares with us only the fact that the purchase happened on your Google account, so the unlock can follow your account to new devices. 4. Information stored on your device Your game progress (levels reached, yarn saved, cats adopted, cosmetics, high scores) and ad counters (wins since the last ad, reels watched today) are stored locally on your device using the browser's local storage (or the device's app storage on Android). This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing website data removes it permanently. 5. Children The game is declared for a general audience (13+). It is not directed at children, and no age-based targeting is used. Since we collect no data ourselves, no data of children is collected by us. 6. Permissions The Android build requests network access only so ads and the purchase can work; the storybook itself plays fully offline. The web version uses only standard browser storage to save your progress. 7. Contact If you have any questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email listed on the store page.
